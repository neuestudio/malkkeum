"I'll do a big clean this weekend," and then the weekend is gone. Cleaning feels heavy because **there is too much to do at once**. Split tasks by frequency and ten minutes a day is enough. Copy the list below into your phone notes or calendar reminders, or download the printable version at the bottom.

## Daily (10 minutes)

- [ ] Wipe the sink dry and run hot water for 30 seconds after dishes
- [ ] Squeegee bathroom walls and mirror after showering
- [ ] Take laundry out right away and leave the washer door open
- [ ] Pull back the covers for 30 minutes and air the room for 10
- [ ] Put things on the floor back where they belong

## Weekly (1 hour)

- [ ] Vacuum and mop the floors
- [ ] Wash bedding and towels
- [ ] [Steam clean the microwave](/en/guides/microwave/)
- [ ] [Clean the kitchen sink strainer](/en/guides/sink-drain/)
- [ ] [Citric acid on the toilet](/en/guides/toilet-stain/); wipe sink and faucets
- [ ] Check food dates in the fridge (the day before shopping)
- [ ] [Sanitize cutting boards](/en/guides/cutting-board/)
- [ ] Empty the dishwasher filter

## Monthly (half a day)

- [ ] Clean the [front-load](/en/guides/drum-washer/) or [top-load washer](/en/guides/top-loader/)
- [ ] [Degrease the hood filter and cooktop](/en/guides/range-hood/)
- [ ] Check [grout](/en/guides/tile-grout/) and [silicone](/en/guides/silicone-mold/) for mold
- [ ] [Descale the kettle](/en/guides/electric-kettle/)
- [ ] [Clean the dishwasher](/en/guides/dishwasher/)
- [ ] [Vacuum the mattress](/en/guides/mattress/)
- [ ] Check and replace moisture absorbers and deodorizers

## Every season (one weekend day)

- [ ] [Window screens](/en/guides/window-screen/) and [tracks](/en/guides/window-rail/)
- [ ] [Deep clean the fridge](/en/guides/fridge/)
- [ ] [Tidy and deodorize the shoe cabinet](/en/guides/shoe-cabinet/)
- [ ] [Deep clean a fabric sofa](/en/guides/fabric-sofa/) or [condition a leather one](/en/guides/leather-sofa/)
- [ ] [Descale the shower head](/en/guides/shower-head/)
- [ ] Swap seasonal clothes and bedding ([storage tips](/en/magazine/summer-bedding-storage/))

## Extra jobs by season

| Season | Add |
|---|---|
| Spring | Screens and tracks after pollen and dust season, wash winter bedding, clean and store the humidifier |
| Summer | [AC filter](/en/guides/aircon-filter/) every 2 weeks, extra bathroom mold care, dehumidify |
| Autumn | [Store the AC](/en/magazine/aircon-storage/), [check the boiler](/en/magazine/boiler-check/), [clean the humidifier](/en/magazine/humidifier-prep/), [prepare for condensation](/en/magazine/condensation-mold/) |
| Winter | Wipe window condensation each morning, air the rooms 2–3 times a day, change humidifier water daily |

## Tips for sticking with it

- **One area at a time.** Bathroom today, kitchen tomorrow keeps it light.
- **Keep tools where you use them.** A squeegee in the bathroom and cloths in a kitchen drawer means less effort to start.
- **Consistency over perfection.** Skipping a weekly task one week is fine. Just pick it up the next.
