You spray bleach on bathroom mold, the water spots will not budge, so you add some citric acid. People who clean diligently have often done something like this. But it is one of the most dangerous cleaning mistakes you can make at home.

## Never mix these

| Combination | What it creates | When it happens |
|---|---|---|
| Bleach + acidic cleaners | Chlorine gas | Using vinegar, citric acid or acidic toilet cleaner after mold removal |
| Bleach + ammonia | Chloramine gas | Bleaching the toilet, then using an ammonia glass cleaner |
| Bleach + alcohol | Harmful chlorinated compounds | Using rubbing alcohol and bleach on the same cloth |
| Hydrogen peroxide + vinegar | Peracetic acid (irritating, corrosive) | Mixing them in one bottle as a stain remover |
| Different drain cleaners | Sudden heat and reaction | Pouring a second product when the first did not work |

Chlorine and chloramine gases strongly irritate the eyes and throat even in small amounts, and in a small, poorly ventilated bathroom they can cause breathing difficulties.

## Harmless but pointless

**Baking soda + vinegar** fizzes impressively, but the alkali and acid neutralize each other and both lose strength. Unless you need the physical action of the foam, as in a drain, they clean better used separately.

## Rules for cleaning safely

1. **One cleaner at a time.** Rinse thoroughly before switching, and ideally use the other product on a different day.
2. **Read the label.** Take extra care with products marked chlorine, acidic or do not mix.
3. **Ventilate first.** Open the door and switch on the fan before cleaning the bathroom.
4. **Dilute bleach in cold water.** Hot water releases more chlorine.
5. **Gloves and mask.** Protect your skin and lungs.
6. **Never decant.** Someone else may not know what is in the bottle and mix it.

## If you have already mixed them

If you smell something sharp or your eyes sting, **leave the room immediately, open windows and get fresh air.** Do not go back in to clean up; wait until the gas clears. If coughing continues or breathing is difficult, call your local emergency number without hesitation.

Each cleaner has its own job: chlorine for mold, acids for limescale, alkalis for grease. Remember to use them **separately and in order**, and cleaning becomes both safer and more effective.
