The washer cleaner aisle can be overwhelming. But there are really only three things to decide: **the ingredient, the form and your washer.**

## 1. Ingredient: oxygen vs chlorine

| | Oxygen | Chlorine |
|---|---|---|
| How it works | Oxygen bubbles lift grime off | Chlorine kills and bleaches mold |
| Pros | Residue visibly comes out; low odor | Strong disinfection; works in cold water |
| Cons | Works best in warm water; takes time | Strong smell; dangerous with acids |
| Best for | Regular monthly cleaning | Occasional use for bad odors |

If you are starting out, choose **oxygen**. You can see the residue that comes loose, so it is easy to tell whether cleaning worked, and it is relatively safe if it meets other cleaners.

## 2. Form: powder vs liquid

Powders are mostly oxygen-based, easy to measure and cheaper. Liquids dissolve easily, even on cold cycles, but you usually get less for the same price.

## 3. Your washer

- **Front-loaders** need low-foam products. Look for "HE" or "suitable for front-loaders" on the package.
- **Top-loaders** fill with water and soak, so oxygen powders work especially well.
- **Manufacturer recommendations:** some manuals recommend a specific product or ingredient. It is worth checking.
