  - title: Start when the sun is not strong
    body: In direct sun the soapy water dries too fast and leaves streaks. Clean on a cloudy day or in the morning or evening when the sun is off the glass.
  - title: Dust the frame and glass first
    body: Wipe dust off the glass from the top with a dry cloth and clear the window track. Spraying water onto dust turns it into muddy water that spreads.
  - title: Spray and wipe with soapy water
    body: Mix 500ml water with 2 or 3 drops of dish soap in a spray bottle. More soap leaves a film. Spray from the top down and wipe with a microfiber cloth to loosen the grime.
  - title: Pull the water off with a squeegee
    body: Place the squeegee at the top edge and pull straight down in one stroke. Wipe the blade with a dry cloth after every pass, and overlap each pass slightly so nothing is missed.
  - title: Finish the edges and spots
    body: Dry the edges and corners with a dry microfiber cloth. For leftover fingerprints or grease, dampen a cloth with ethanol and water mixed 1:1 and wipe just those spots.
  - title: Clean outside glass only within reach
    body: Clean only what you can reach without leaning out. With sliding windows, slide each pane over to reach most of the outer surface. Leave high outside glass to building management or professionals.
  - title: Let it dry, then insulate
    body: Moisture trapped under the film leads to mold and water spots. Let the glass dry for about 30 minutes, then follow the product instructions, usually misting the glass and pressing the smooth side of the bubble wrap on.
cautions:
  - Ethanol is flammable. Open the window and keep it away from stoves and heaters.
  - Never lean out of the window or place a chair or ladder by an open window.
  - Ethanol and scouring pads can damage coated or film-covered glass. Use only soapy water and a soft cloth.
  - On very cold days, hot water on outside glass can crack it. Use lukewarm water.

  - Wipe the inside horizontally and the outside vertically so you can tell which side a streak is on.
  - Wash microfiber cloths without fabric softener, which leaves an oily film on glass.

## Why do windows streak?

Most streaks come from **too much soap or water drying too fast**. Strong soap leaves a film, and wiping in direct sun leaves rings as droplets dry. Stick to **weak soapy water, shade and a squeegee** and the glass comes out much clearer.

## Streaks by type

| Mark | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cloudy film | Too much soap or softener in the cloth | Wipe again with plain water, then squeegee |
| Stripes | Water on the squeegee blade, drying in sun | Wipe the blade every pass, redo in shade |
| Fingerprints, grease | Skin oil, cooking splatter | Ethanol and water 1:1 on a cloth, spot only |
| White water spots | Mineral deposits from rain or tap water | Citric acid water (1 tsp in 200ml), then rinse |

## Why clean before insulating?

The gap between bubble wrap and glass collects condensation all winter. Dust and grime under it become **black mold spots**, which is also why glass often looks stained when the film comes off in spring.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is newspaper better for glass?**
Newspaper absorbs water well, which is why people used it. But the ink can rub off onto seals and hands. A microfiber cloth and squeegee are enough.

**Do I need a glass cleaner?**
A few drops of dish soap are enough. Add ethanol only for greasy kitchen windows.
