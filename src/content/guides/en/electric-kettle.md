## What the white flakes are

The white film or flakes floating in your kettle are limescale: calcium and magnesium from tap water that harden as it boils. It is not harmful, but as it builds up, the kettle takes longer to boil and tea tastes dull. Limescale is alkaline, so it dissolves easily in citric acid.

## Can I use vinegar?

Vinegar works on the same principle. Mix water and vinegar 3:1 and use the same method. The smell tends to linger in the kettle, though, so you will need to rinse more times. Odorless citric acid is much easier.

## FAQ

**There is a rainbow sheen inside my kettle.**
That is stainless steel reacting with heat and minerals. It usually fades along with the scale when you use citric acid, and it is not rust.
