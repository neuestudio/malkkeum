    body: Mix 2–3 drops of dish soap into 500 ml of lukewarm water, dip a microfiber cloth, wring it out very well and gently wipe the armrests and seats where hand marks build up.

    body: Wipe off the soap with a cloth dampened in clean water and immediately dry with a dry cloth. Water left on leather causes marks.

    body: When fully dry, put a pea-sized amount of conditioner on a cloth and spread it thinly in circles. After 10 minutes, buff lightly with a dry cloth to remove the excess and reveal a soft sheen.

## Treat leather like skin

Leather is processed animal hide, so like our own skin it needs both **cleansing and moisturizing**. Harsh wiping or strong cleaners remove its natural oils, the surface stiffens, and eventually it cracks and peels. The rule for leather sofas is "clean gently, always condition."

## If it is faux leather

Faux leather (PU or PVC) does not need conditioning, but it is just as sensitive to alcohol and harsh cleaners. Wipe with mild soapy water and dry immediately, then finish with a dry cloth instead of conditioner. If the surface has started to peel, a cover or replacement is the realistic option.

## FAQ

**Can I use hand cream instead of conditioner?**
Hand cream contains fragrance and water, which can leave marks. Leather conditioner is the safer choice.
