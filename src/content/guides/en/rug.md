## Rugs hold more than you think

A rug catches floor dust, hair, crumbs and pet fur. It helps keep dust out of the air, but **if you do not remove it regularly**, it becomes a dust reservoir. Vacuuming properly twice a week makes a visible difference.

## Dealing with stains

| Stain | What to do |
|---|---|
| Coffee, juice | Absorb, then blot with cold water and mild detergent |
| Oil, sauce | Absorb, then blot with slightly stronger dish soap solution |
| Milk, blood | Absorb, then always cold water; never hot |
| Pet accidents | Absorb, enzyme cleaner, dry completely |

If you live with pets, also see [cleaning a home with pets](/en/magazine/pet-home-cleaning/).

## FAQ

**Can I use a steam cleaner?**
On synthetic rugs, brief use is fine, but wool rugs can shrink with heat and moisture. Always dry thoroughly afterwards.
