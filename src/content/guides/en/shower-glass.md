## Why shower glass clouds up so fast

Shower glass is hit by hot water every day and left to dry, again and again. Each drying droplet leaves **tap water minerals as white spots**, and a thin layer of shampoo and soap oil settles on top, creating a cloudy film. That is why shower glass should be cleaned in the order **soap scum (oil) → hard water (minerals)**.

## Why use plastic wrap?

Glass is vertical, so cleaners run straight off. To dissolve mineral deposits the citric acid has to stay in contact, and plastic wrap keeps it from running or drying. Crumpled up afterwards, it doubles as a gentle scrubber.

## The caulk and track below

Black mold often grows on the silicone and sliding track at the bottom of the door. Citric acid does not remove mold, so treat it separately on another day with the [bathroom silicone mold removal](/en/guides/silicone-mold/) method.

## FAQ

**Can I use vinegar?**
Yes, mix vinegar and water 1:1 and use the same method. Turn on the fan and rinse well, as the smell lingers.
