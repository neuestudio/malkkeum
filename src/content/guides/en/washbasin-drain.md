## Two sources of sink smell

Bathroom sink odor usually comes from **hair and soap residue tangled under the stopper** and from **grime inside the overflow hole** that you cannot see. Clean the drain but skip the overflow and the smell will linger.

## If the water drains slowly

Bathroom sink clogs are almost always hair tangled with soap residue. Before pouring in a liquid drain cleaner, try pulling it out with a **drain hair removal tool**. That usually solves it and is kinder to your pipes than chemicals.

## FAQ

**Is it the same as a kitchen sink?**
The principle is the same, but kitchens clog with grease while bathroom sinks clog with hair and soap, so pulling debris out matters more. For kitchens, see [how to get rid of kitchen sink drain odor](/en/guides/sink-drain/).
