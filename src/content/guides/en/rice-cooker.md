    body: With the pot removed, wipe away rice grains on the heating plate and rim with a dry cloth. Debris on the plate stops the pot sitting flat, which can leave rice undercooked.

## The real cause of rice smells

If rice smells sour or musty, suspect **the lid** before the pot. Every time you cook, starchy water rises with the steam and dries a little on the steam vent, inner lid and rubber gasket. That smell then travels back into the rice with the steam next time.

## Gaskets are consumables

The rubber gasket of a pressure rice cooker hardens and loosens over time from constant steam and pressure. If you hear steam escaping while cooking or the rice does not taste as good, it is time for a new gasket. Many manufacturers suggest replacing it roughly once a year; check your manual.

## FAQ

**The coating on my inner pot is peeling. Can I keep using it?**
A peeling pot makes rice stick and is harder to clean. Most manufacturers sell inner pots separately, so we recommend replacing it.
