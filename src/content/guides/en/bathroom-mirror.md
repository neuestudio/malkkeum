## Water spots and soap film are different

Mirror marks come in two kinds. **Round white spots** are minerals left behind as water dries; they dissolve in citric acid. **A cloudy haze** is soap scum made from the oils in shampoo and soap. If there is a lot of haze, wipe the mirror with soapy water before using the citric acid spray.

## The edges have turned black

Dark marks spreading in from the edges are corrosion of the silver backing, known as desilvering. Once it appears, it cannot be wiped away. Keeping water from collecting at the edges and spraying cleaners only in the center of the mirror is the best prevention.

## FAQ

**Can I use vinegar instead?**
Yes. Mix vinegar and water 1:1 and use it the same way. Ventilate well, as the smell lingers.
