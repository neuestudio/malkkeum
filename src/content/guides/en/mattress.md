  - title: Strip the bed and vacuum
    body: Remove all covers and pads and put them in the wash. Vacuum the top, sides and seams of the mattress slowly, 3–4 seconds per stroke, so dust deep in the fibers comes out too.
  - title: Blot the stains
    body: Mix a little mild detergent into cold water, dampen a white towel and dab the stain from the outside in. Rubbing spreads the stain and roughens the fabric.
  - title: Remove the detergent
    body: Dab again with a towel dampened in clean water to lift the detergent, then press a dry towel down firmly to absorb the moisture. The key is keeping water out of the mattress core.
  - title: Sprinkle baking soda
    body: Sift baking soda evenly over the whole mattress and leave it for at least an hour. It absorbs sweat odors and moisture. Wait until any spot-cleaned areas are completely dry first.
  - title: Vacuum again and dry
    body: Vacuum up all the baking soda, open the windows and run a fan until everything is fully dry. Only put the bedding back on when it is completely dry, or mold can grow.
cautions:
  - Hydrogen peroxide and oxygen-based stain removers can bleach fabric. Test low on the side first.
  - Holding a steam cleaner in one spot for long traps moisture in the foam and springs. Keep it moving, briefly and widely.
  - A hair dryer held close on hot can melt or shrink the fabric.
tips:
  - One waterproof mattress protector saves dozens of stain cleanups.
  - Rotate the mattress head to foot every three months to prevent sagging on one side. Flip it too if it is double-sided.

## What dust mites love

House dust mites feed on dead skin and thrive in **warm, humid places**. A mattress gets body heat and sweat every night, which makes it one of their favorite homes. Simply vacuuming out dust and skin flakes, drying out moisture and washing covers often makes a big difference.

## If yellow stains are old

Old sweat stains are body oils that have oxidized and turned yellow. If mild detergent does not lift them, use a fabric stain remover as directed and dab in several short rounds. Pouring on a lot at once makes the stain spread into a ring.

## FAQ

**Does sunlight kill dust mites?**
Sun and airflow reduce moisture, making life hard for mites. If moving the mattress outside is impractical, opening the windows and drying it with a fan is effective too.
