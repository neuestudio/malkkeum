## Induction and radiant: same cleaning

Both have a **ceramic glass** surface. The difference is how they heat: induction heats only the pan, and the glass warms from the pan, while radiant cooktops glow red themselves. That is why spills on radiant cooktops burn on faster. Clean them the same way, but **wait longer** for a radiant cooktop to cool.

## Preventing scratches matters most

Scratches on a glass cooktop never go away, and stains cling to them. So rather than scrubbing hard, **soak, scrape and wipe**. For thick baked-on spills, lay a damp cloth over them for 5 minutes before using the scraper.

## FAQ

**Can I use baking soda?**
Rubbing dry powder can leave fine scratches. If you want to use it, dissolve it fully in water and wipe gently with a soft sponge.
