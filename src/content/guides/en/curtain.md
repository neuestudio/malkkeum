    body: Fold the curtains along their pleats, put them in a large laundry bag and wash on a wool or delicate cycle with mild detergent in cold water (30°C / 86°F or below). Wash one or two panels at a time to limit creasing.

    body: Put the hooks back and hang the curtains straight on the rail. The weight of the water pulls out the creases naturally, and with the window open they dry in half a day.

## Curtains catch more dust than you think

Curtains are the first filter for dust and fine particles coming through the window. They also absorb cooking smells and grease, so curtains that have not been washed in a long time can be the source of room odor. Washing them after spring dust season or before closing the windows in autumn is ideal.

## By material

| Material | Washing | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Polyester | Machine, delicate cycle | Keep spin short |
| Linen / cotton | Hand wash or delicate cycle | Shrinks easily; cold water only |
| Blackout (coated) | Hand wash or dry clean | Coating can crack |
| Velvet / silk | Dry clean | Deforms when washed |

## FAQ

**I have no washing machine. Can I wash them in the bathtub?**
Yes. Soak them in lukewarm water with mild detergent for 30 minutes, press gently with your feet, rinse two or three times, press out the water and hang them on the rail. Wringing them sets deep creases.
