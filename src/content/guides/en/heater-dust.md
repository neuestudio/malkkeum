  - title: Get between the fins of an oil radiator
    body: Ribbed oil radiators collect the most dust between their fins. Slide a soft brush between the fins, sweep from top to bottom, and vacuum up the dust on the floor.

    body: Open a window slightly and run the heater for 10 to 15 minutes. Any remaining dust burns off during this time. If the smell keeps fading, everything is normal.

## Why does it smell like burning?

Electric and fan heaters sit unused all summer while dust settles on the intake, the grille and around the element. The first time you switch one on in winter, that dust touches the hot element and burns, which causes the **acrid smell**. It usually fades within minutes, but dusting first means less smell and less dust blown around the room.

## Normal smells and smells that mean switch off

| Smell or sign | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A brief dusty burning smell on first use | Built-up dust | Open a window and run it 10 to 15 minutes |
| A burning smell that lasts for days | Heavy dust inside or a faulty part | Stop using it and get it serviced |
| Melting plastic smell, smoke, crackling | Cord, plug or internal part overheating | **Switch off and unplug immediately** |

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I wash the filter with water?**
It depends on the model. Only wash it if the manual says it is washable, and let it dry fully in the shade before refitting it. A damp filter can damage the heater.

**Is it the same for carbon or halogen heaters?**
Yes, dust the casing and grille the same way. The heating lamps break easily, so keep the brush on the outside of the grille and away from the lamps.
