## What the grime is

The grayish grime on sofas is mostly **skin oil and sweat** from hands and heads, mixed with dust. That is why it concentrates on armrests, headrests and the front edge of the seat. Oil does not come out with water alone and needs the surfactants in detergent, but water must not soak into the fabric, which is why we use **foam**.

## If it does not come out the first time

Old grime rarely lifts in one round. Letting it dry fully and repeating the process is gentler on the fabric, and gives a better result, than scrubbing hard once. If marks remain, try a fabric stain remover on those spots.

## FAQ

**Can I use a steam cleaner?**
Steam loosens grime quickly, but it leaves a lot of moisture in the fabric and cushions. If you use one, keep it brief and always dry thoroughly with a fan.
