## Two kinds of grout grime

Grout looks dirty for two reasons. **Gray or yellowish grime** is soap scum from shampoo, soap and body oils, and it loosens with mildly alkaline baking soda. **Black spots** are mold that has grown into the grout. Scrubbing will not remove them; they need a chlorine remover. Tackling them in that order saves effort and gives a cleaner result.

## Why be careful with acids?

Citric acid is popular for bathrooms, but it does not suit cement-based grout. Acid slowly dissolves cement, roughening the surface, and those rough pits let grime and mold in more easily. Even when using citric acid on tile surfaces for limescale, rinse it off grout lines right away.

## FAQ

**Can I use an electric scrubber or drill brush?**
Yes, it makes large floors much easier. Just use a medium setting; very stiff bristles held too long can wear the grout down.
