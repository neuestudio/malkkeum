  - title: Mix the solution

  - title: Spray and cover with paper towels
    body: Spray the greasy tiles, press paper towels over them and spray again so they stick. After 10 minutes the sticky grease softens.
  - title: Wipe with a sponge
    body: Peel off the paper towels, wiping as you go, then wipe from top to bottom with a soft sponge. The grease dissolves and the tile color returns.
  - title: Scrub the grout
    body: Grease soaks into grout lines. Dip a toothbrush in the solution and scrub along the grout.
  - title: Rinse and dry
    body: Wipe two or three times with a cloth dampened in clean water to remove the soap, then dry with a dry cloth. Leftover soap attracts dust.

tips:

## Why kitchen wall grease gets sticky

Cooking fumes turn into tiny droplets of oil that settle on nearby walls. At first it is a thin film, but mixed with dust and heated again and again, it hardens into **sticky yellow grime**. Grease is acidic, so mildly alkaline baking soda and detergent dissolve it well.

## For old, hardened grease

Grease that has built up for months may not come off in one go. Spray the solution and **cover with plastic wrap for 20–30 minutes** to soften it further. If some still remains, dissolve oxygen bleach in warm water and use it the same way, wearing rubber gloves.

## FAQ

**Should I clean the hood filter too?**
Yes. If the wall is greasy, the hood filter will be too. Doing [range hood filter degreasing](/en/guides/range-hood/) on the same day finishes the kitchen in one go.
