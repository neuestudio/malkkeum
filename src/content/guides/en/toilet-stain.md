## What the yellow stain really is

Most yellow toilet stains are **urine scale**: calcium and uric acid from urine hardened together with minerals in tap water. It collects at the waterline and under the rim. Because it is alkaline, alkaline cleaners and bleach do little; it needs an **acid such as citric acid** to soften.

Black or pink stains, on the other hand, are mold or bacteria. A chlorine cleaner works on those, but never use it on the same day as citric acid.

## Do not forget under the rim

The inside of the rim, where the water comes out, is hard to see, so it collects the most scale and mold. Look with a small hand mirror and it is often dirtier than you expect. Spray citric acid all around the underside and scrub with a curved toilet brush.

## FAQ

**I heard pouring cola works.**
Cola is mildly acidic, so it helps a little with light stains. But it leaves sticky sugar behind and is weaker than citric acid. Citric acid is cheap and reliable.
