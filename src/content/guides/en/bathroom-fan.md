  - title: Turn off the power
    body: Switch off the fan and, if possible, turn off the bathroom circuit at the breaker panel. Reaching in while it runs can injure your hand on the blades.
  - title: Remove the cover
    body: Standing on a stable step stool, hold the edges of the cover and pull down gently to reveal the spring clips on each side. Squeeze the clips together to release the cover. Wear a mask, as dust will fall.
  - title: Wash the cover
    body: Wash the cover in lukewarm soapy water, scrubbing between the slats with a toothbrush, then rinse. Let it dry completely in the shade.
  - title: Remove dust from the blades
    body: Vacuum dust from the fan blades and housing with a brush attachment, then wipe off the rest with a dry cloth. Never spray water or use a wet cloth on the motor housing.
  - title: Refit and test
    body: When the cover is dry, squeeze the clips and push it back in. Turn the power back on and switch on the fan; hold a sheet of tissue near the cover to check it gets pulled up.
cautions:
  - The fan housing contains electrical parts. Do not spray water or cleaner on it.
  - Bathroom floors are slippery. Dry the floor, use a non-slip step and, if possible, have someone hold it.
  - If the cover will not come off easily or is fixed with screws, do not force it; check the product manual.

## The fan decides whether mold returns

Bathroom mold ultimately comes down to **how quickly moisture leaves the room**. When the fan cover is caked in dust, air cannot get out, and the bathroom stays damp for hours after a shower. If mold keeps coming back no matter how much remover you use, clean the fan first.

## Why there is so much dust

Along with moisture, a bathroom fan pulls in towel lint, hair and cosmetic powder. Mixed with humidity, this dust sticks to the cover slats and will not come off with a dry cloth. Taking the cover off and washing it is the most reliable approach.

## FAQ

**Does running the fan a lot cost much?**
Bathroom fans use relatively little power. Running one for 30–60 minutes after a shower is well worth it compared with the effort of cleaning mold.
