## Two kinds of grime, two cleaners

Bathtubs collect two types of grime. The **gray or yellow ring** is an oily mix of body oils and soap, and mild detergent handles it well. **White marks** are minerals from tap water, which dissolve in an acid such as citric acid. Rather than hoping one cleaner does everything, start with the oily grime and work in order.

## Know your tub material

| Material | Features | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Acrylic | Light and warm; most common | Scratches easily |
| Enamel (porcelain) | Hard and glossy | Strong acids and abrasives |
| Solid surface | Smooth, matte | Acidic cleaners |

Most home bathtubs are acrylic, and a soft sponge with mild detergent is the safest choice.

## FAQ

**My bathtub has turned yellow all over.**
If an old acrylic tub is yellow all over, the material itself may be discolored rather than dirty. Cleaning will not reverse it; refinishing or replacement is the option.
