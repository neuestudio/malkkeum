## What causes shoe cabinet smell

The smell comes from **bacteria feeding on sweat and dead skin** inside shoes, combined with **moisture** trapped in a closed space. Cleaning the cabinet helps, but if damp shoes keep going in, the smell returns quickly. Drying shoes before storing them is just as important as cleaning.

## If you see mold

White or black spots on shelf corners or walls are mold. Wipe them away with alcohol and keep a moisture absorber in the cabinet. Mold is most common during rainy seasons and winter condensation, so open the cabinet wide once a month then.

## FAQ

**Can I just use an air freshener?**
Air fresheners only cover the smell; they do not remove the cause. Clean and dry first, then add a scent if you like.
