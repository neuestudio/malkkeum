## Why check them before use?

A heated water mat keeps water in its tank and hoses, so last winter's water leaves **scale and a musty smell**. An electric blanket stored folded can **kink its heating wires**, and a kinked wire can get dangerously hot in one spot. Spend 20 to 40 minutes on it before the first cold night.

## Which one do you have?

| | Heated water mat | Electric blanket |
|---|---|---|
| How it heats | Warm water circulates through hoses | Current runs through heating wires |
| Key check | Drain and refill the water | Look for kinked wires and scorch marks |
| Washing | Wipe the mat, wash only the cover | Only if labeled washable, controller removed |
| Storage | Fully drained | Rolled, never folded |

## Stop using it if

- One spot gets unusually hot, or it smells like burning
- The controller or plug gets hot or discolored
- The water mat leaks or the heater unit makes unusual noises

Cleaning will not fix these. Unplug it and contact the manufacturer for service.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use filtered water in a water mat?**
Recommended water varies by model. Use the water named in the manual, and never add additives or disinfectants it does not mention.

**Why not fold an electric blanket for storage?**
Folding the same spot repeatedly can damage the wires. Roll it up and keep heavy things off it.
