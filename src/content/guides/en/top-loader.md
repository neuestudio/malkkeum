steps:
  - title: Wash the lint filter
    body: Remove the lint filter on the inside wall of the tub, shake out lint and hair and scrub it with a toothbrush. A clogged filter lets loosened residue settle back onto clothes.
  - title: Fill with warm water
    body: Fill the tub to the highest level with water at 40–50°C (104–122°F), using the warm setting or a bucket. Oxygen bleach barely dissolves in cold water and loses its power.
  - title: Add oxygen bleach and agitate briefly
    body: Add washer cleaner as directed on the package, or 300–500 g (about 1 1/2–2 cups) of sodium percarbonate. Run a wash or soak cycle for 5 minutes to dissolve it, then pause.
  - title: Soak for 3–5 hours
    body: Close the lid and leave it for 3–5 hours. Black mold and detergent residue from the hidden outer tub start floating to the surface.
  - title: Skim out the residue
    body: Scoop out as much floating residue as you can with the skimmer. Draining it all can clog the hose and drain, so this step matters. Agitate for another 5 minutes and skim again.
  - title: Rinse and dry
    body: Drain and spin, then refill and run a rinse cycle. Repeat 2–3 times until no more residue appears, then leave the lid open for several hours.
cautions:
  - Water above 60°C (140°F) can warp washer parts. Stay at 50°C (122°F) or below.
  - Bleach (chlorine) together with citric acid or vinegar (acids) releases toxic gas. Use one type at a time.
  - Wear rubber gloves with oxygen bleach and keep the powder away from your eyes.
  - If your manual has its own tub-cleaning instructions, follow them first.
tips:
  - A machine cleaned for the first time releases a lot of residue. Do not try to finish in one go; repeat a week later.
  - Take laundry out right away and leave the lid open, and you can clean half as often.
  - Using more detergent and softener than recommended leaves residue that feeds mold.

## How it differs from a front-loader

A top-loader can fill its tub completely, so **soaking in warm water and lifting the grime off** is the most effective approach. A front-loader uses little water, so this method does not work there and foam can overflow. For front-loaders, follow the [front-load washer cleaning guide](/en/guides/drum-washer/).

## FAQ

**The residue never stops coming.**
If the machine has not been cleaned in a long time, one round will not be enough. If residue still appears after three rinses, stop for the day and repeat a few days later. The second round releases much less.
