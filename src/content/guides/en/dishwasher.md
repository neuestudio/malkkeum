steps:
  - title: Remove the lower rack
    body: Turn off the dishwasher and pull the lower rack all the way out. You should see a cylindrical filter in the center of the floor.
  - title: Remove and wash the filter
    body: Twist the cylindrical filter counterclockwise and lift it out; the flat mesh below usually comes with it. Shake out food scraps, scrub the mesh with a toothbrush and dish soap and rinse under running water.
  - title: Clear the spray arm holes
    body: Remove the lower and upper spray arms, hold them up to the light and clear any blocked holes with a toothpick. Blocked holes leave spots the water never reaches.
  - title: Wipe the door gasket and edges
    body: The rubber door gasket and the bottom edge of the door never get sprayed, so debris and mold collect there. Clean them with a soapy cloth and toothbrush.
  - title: Reassemble and run empty
    body: Lock the filter and arms firmly back in place, add dishwasher cleaner and run the hottest cycle empty. Use citric acid only if your manufacturer allows it.
  - title: Leave the door open to dry
    body: When the cycle ends, leave the door slightly open until the interior is completely dry. Removing moisture stops odors returning.
cautions:
  - Regular dish soap in a dishwasher foams over and can cause breakdowns and leaks. Use it only for hand-washing the filter.
  - Bleach damages the stainless interior and rubber parts. Do not use it in a dishwasher.
  - Running without the filter lets debris into the pump. Double-check that it is back in.
  - Cycles and approved cleaners vary by manufacturer. Read your manual first.
tips:
  - Scrape off large food scraps before loading and filter cleaning gets much easier. There is no need to pre-rinse.
  - If dishes come out with white spots, check whether the rinse aid has run out.
  - If you wait a few days to fill a load, leave the door slightly open to reduce odor.

## If your dishwasher smells

The cause is almost always **food trapped in the filter**. The filter is not flushed clean each cycle; debris keeps accumulating, and within a week or so a sour smell appears. If it smells, clean the filter before running a cleaning cycle.

## If dishes are not getting clean

When food is left on dishes, check three things: whether the filter is clogged, whether the spray arm holes are blocked, and whether a dish is stopping the arms from turning. One of these is almost always the answer.

## FAQ

**Can I use citric acid?**
Citric acid helps with limescale, but some manufacturers advise against it because of possible damage to rubber parts. Check your manual, and if in doubt, use a dedicated dishwasher cleaner.
