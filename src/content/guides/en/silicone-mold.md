## Why scrubbing does not work

Black mold on silicone is not only on the surface; it has **rooted into the silicone itself**. Brushing fades the top layer, but it turns black again soon after. A chlorine remover has to stay in contact for a while to soak in, break down the pigment and kill the mold.

## If it will not go away

If black spots remain after two or three rounds, the mold has grown deep into the caulk. At that point the answer is **recaulking**, not cleaning. Cut away the old silicone, disinfect the joint with mold remover, let it dry completely and apply new anti-mold bathroom silicone. If you are not comfortable doing it yourself, hire a caulking professional.

## FAQ

**Can I use diluted bleach?**
Yes. Follow the mold-removal dilution on the label, mix it with cold water and soak paper towels to lay over the caulk. Liquid runs off vertical surfaces easily, though, which is why a gel is more convenient.
