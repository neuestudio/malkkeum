## Why cleaning the basket is not enough

An air fryer cooks by circulating hot air fast. Grease that splatters from food lands on the **heating element and fan area above the basket**. You may wash the basket every time, but this part is hard to see and easy to ignore for months. Each time that grease heats up, it smokes, smells burnt and flavors your food.

## Making the coating last

Once the basket coating starts to peel, food sticks and cleaning gets harder. **Soaking and wiping gently** is the key. Use silicone tongs instead of metal ones and avoid knocking the basket when shaking it. Whether the basket is dishwasher-safe varies by model, so check the manual.

## FAQ

**The inside keeps smelling.**
If the smell remains after cleaning the element, put a heatproof dish of water with lemon slices in the basket, run it at a low temperature for a few minutes, then wipe it out. If it still smells, have it checked by the manufacturer.
