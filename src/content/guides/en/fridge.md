## Where fridge odors come from

Most fridge smells come from **spilled liquids and spoiled food**, especially water pooling in the bottom of the vegetable drawer and sauce spilled under the door shelves. If the smell is strong, check these two spots before reaching for a deodorizer.

## If the freezer has thick frost

Frost more than about 1 cm thick lowers cooling efficiency and raises your electricity bill. Move the food out, switch the freezer off and leave the door open to let it melt naturally. Never chip at it with a knife or pick; you can puncture the cooling lines. Most modern fridges defrost automatically, so if frost keeps building up, check whether the door seal is loose.

## FAQ

**Can I wipe it with vinegar?**
Diluted vinegar works fine, but the smell may linger, so baking soda water and alcohol are easier for fridges.
